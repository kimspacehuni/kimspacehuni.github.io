HRL-LoRA: Hierarchical RL for Consistent Subtask Discovery in VLAs
Gyeonghun Kim
Course Project, Stochastic Reinforcement Learning, SNU, 2025
PDF

Proposed a hierarchical framework that discovers robotic subtasks without manual supervision by attaching independent LoRA experts to a frozen VLA backbone (π₀) and training a PPO router to select experts. Introduced a consistency-aware reward to induce temporally coherent, interpretable subtask segmentation, and showed that entropy regularization is critical to avoid degenerate routing collapse.
